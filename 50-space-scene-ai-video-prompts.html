Tips for Best Results

✦
Name the spacecraft or setting specifically — "Mars rover" or "space station cupola" gives far more accurate results than just "spaceship".
✦
Describe the light source precisely — "harsh unfiltered sunlight", "earthshine", "plasma glow" — space shots live or die on their lighting logic.
✦
Camera position matters more than usual here — helmet POV, station window, or exterior craft-to-craft each produce a completely different feel from the same scene.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI tools work with these prompts?
These prompts are optimised for Kling AI, Runway Gen-3, and Sora. They also work well with Pika, LumaLabs, and most other text-to-video AI tools. The spaceflight and cinematography language is universally understood by all major AI video models.
Can I modify these prompts for my own scenes?
Absolutely. The prompts are designed to be templates. Replace the aircraft type or setting with your own while keeping the camera position, lighting, and motion description — those elements are what make the output look cinematic.
Why do some of my space shots look off?
AI video models can struggle with zero-gravity physics and lighting if the prompt is vague. Being specific about setting, gravity environment, and light source (direct sun, earthshine, plasma) significantly improves accuracy.
Do I need aerospace knowledge to use these prompts?
No. You can copy and paste any prompt directly into your AI tool without understanding the technical terms. Each prompt already includes the space-specific detail needed for an accurate result.
