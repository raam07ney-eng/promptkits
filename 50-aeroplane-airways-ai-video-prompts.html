Tips for Best Results

✦
Name the aircraft type specifically — "wide-body airliner" or "P-51 Mustang" gives far more accurate results than just "plane".
✦
Describe the light and sky condition — "golden hour", "overcast", "clear blue at altitude" — aviation shots live or die on their sky.
✦
Camera position matters more than usual here — ground level, cockpit POV, or aerial-to-aerial each produce a completely different feel from the same aircraft.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI tools work with these prompts?
These prompts are optimised for Kling AI, Runway Gen-3, and Sora. They also work well with Pika, LumaLabs, and most other text-to-video AI tools. The aviation and cinematography language is universally understood by all major AI video models.
Can I modify these prompts for my own scenes?
Absolutely. The prompts are designed to be templates. Replace the aircraft type or setting with your own while keeping the camera position, lighting, and motion description — those elements are what make the output look cinematic.
Why do some of my aviation shots look off?
AI video models can struggle with aircraft proportions and flight physics if the prompt is vague. Being specific about aircraft type, camera distance, and flight phase (takeoff, cruise, landing) significantly improves accuracy.
Do I need aviation knowledge to use these prompts?
No. You can copy and paste any prompt directly into your AI tool without understanding the technical terms. Each prompt already includes the aviation-specific detail needed for an accurate result.
